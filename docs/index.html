BioReview-Bench

A silver-standard benchmark for measuring how closely an AI-generated biomedical review overlaps concerns recorded in published peer review.

Claim boundary. The reference concerns, categories, severities, and response-linked stance labels were structured by an LLM and have not yet received independent expert validation. Scores measure overlap with these recorded references; they do not measure scientific truth or certify review quality.
Public boundary. The Hugging Face release is a rights-minimized, non-executable index, not a public test set or open leaderboard. The F1000 DOI-stem audit is a source-specific lower bound: 115 families cross train–validation, 41 cross train–test, and 9 cross validation–test. Development data overlap 49/150 F1000 test articles. At the uncalibrated Jaccard cutoff 0.195, query-time family filtering reduced BM25 F1 from 0.0577 to 0.0148 on the primary full-600 snapshot. The 450-article non-eLife result is post-hoc. See the measurement audit and known issues.

Current evaluation

  • Official frozen v4 score snapshot: 600 articles.
  • Non-eLife analysis: 450-article post-hoc subset, not a replacement snapshot.
  • Jaccard operating-point sweeps are sensitivity diagnostics, not matcher calibration.
  • Historical matcher: unadapted allenai/specter2_base with automatic mean pooling and no recorded adapter/revision.
  • Threshold-aware Hungarian assignment at τ = 0.65: maximize eligible match count first, then total similarity.
  • The threshold has not been independently validated by expert pair judgments, so absolute scores and small gaps are provisional and matcher-dependent.

NeurIPS 2026 review status

The public response reports all three reviewer ratings (2/6), the initial meta-review recommendation, and the revisions made in v4.1. The v4.1.2 hotfix added the public artifact boundary and aggregate measurement audit. v4.1.3 corrects the paired statistical procedure, extends aggregate split/sensitivity checks, and validates the exact public package tree. The conference process was still ongoing when this page was updated; those reviews are not presented as a final decision.

The Hugging Face v4.1.3 release is a clean-history, rights-minimized index. Raw publisher text, normalized concern text, reviewer names/emails, explicit identity fields, and test targets remain private. Concern IDs may retain source-local review ordinals that do not identify a person. There is no public test-scoring or result-submission service.
